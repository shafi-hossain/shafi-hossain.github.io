About

I've spent more than a decade building enterprise software, nearly nine of those years at BJIT, where I moved from senior engineer to principal. Today I lead cross-functional teams through the full delivery cycle: system design, estimation, sprint planning, code and design reviews, and the release itself.

I like work that sits between architecture and people. On one side that means tuning .NET services and database queries for high-concurrency workloads. On the other it means running Scrum well, keeping international clients informed, and helping engineers grow from junior to senior.

I'm a Certified ScrumMaster® and an Oracle Cloud Infrastructure Multicloud Architect Professional.
